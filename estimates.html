PREPARE FOR AN ESTIMATE

Useful information to provide

For a more meaningful discussion, prepare project drawings (if available), specifications, site location, preferred materials, project scope and any known budget constraints.

Actual costs and timeframes may change due to site conditions, design changes, material availability, transport, labour, weather and other project factors.

ASK FOR AN ESTIMATE

Plan with clarity

Good estimating and scheduling are planning tools. They support decision-making but do not replace a site assessment, confirmed scope, supplier quotations or a formal construction agreement.
