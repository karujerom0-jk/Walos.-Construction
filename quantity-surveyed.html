QUANTITY SURVEYING (QS)

Better cost awareness starts with better information.

Quantity surveying is an important part of construction planning. It helps translate drawings and project specifications into measured quantities that can inform material planning, cost estimates and project decisions.

  • Discuss project drawings and available specifications
  • Review measurement and quantity requirements
  • Support material and cost-planning discussions
  • Identify information needed to develop an estimate
The accuracy and detail of any quantity or cost assessment depend on the drawings, specifications, site information and scope provided.
